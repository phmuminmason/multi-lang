ASCII Letter Grade Hack
Ideas and Notes

1. Grade letter depends on Tens digit
	A -> Greater than 8
	B -> 8
	C -> 7
	D -> 6
	F -> Less than 6
2. Sign depends on one's digit range
	+ -> 7..9
	_ -> 4..6
	- -> 0..3

3. ASCII Values
	A -> 65
	B -> 66
	C -> 67
	D -> 68
	F -> 70 [fall through?]

	+ -> 43
	  -> 32
	- -> 45  

	a) Take ten's compliment of grade's significant digit
		i.e. 9 - 10 = 1; $64 + 1 = 65 "A"
			 8 - 10 = 2; $64 + 2 = 66 "B"
			 ...
			 6 - 10 = 4; $64 + 4 = 68 "D"
			 5 - 10 = 5; $64 + 5 = 69 "E" [problem...]
	b) Bit Mask
		0000 -> 0 | "-"
		0001 -> 1 | "-"
		0010 -> 2 | "-"
		0011 -> 3 | "-"
		0100 -> 4 | " "
		0101 -> 5 | " "
		0110 -> 6 | " "
		0111 -> 7 | "+"
		1000 -> 8 | "+"
		1001 -> 9 | "+"

		00xx -> minus
		01xx -> natural
		
		- "Plus" is not so clean, but adding 1 to a Natural cannot change it into a Plus...
		- Add 1 to the whole thing and shift the checks up???

Problems:
1. The fact that grades drop from D to F means adding raw numbers to a constant, then using the result as an ASCII value does not work
2. There is no bit mask that can be applied to sift out each grade sign. Also, the ASCII plus, minus and space characters are not sequential, so the same approach as the letter grade cannot work
